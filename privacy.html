Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

My Student Club ("MSC", "we", "us", or "our") operates mscplacements.com as an employer placement portal designed to facilitate candidate recruitment between companies and trained CA Industrial Trainees & Freshers.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a vacancy or contact us through this website, we collect information necessary to process your recruitment request, including:

  • Your name, company name, work email address, and phone number.
  • Role details, including role title, domain, location, stipend range, and job descriptions.
  • Technical metadata such as IP address and browser user agent for spam prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the submitted information exclusively for employer placement operations:

  • Matching your company's vacancy with pre-screened CA Industrial Trainees and CA Freshers.
  • Communicating with you via email or phone regarding potential candidate profiles.
  • Improving our candidate matching service and employer relations.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade employer data to third parties. Information is only accessed by authorized members of the MSC placements team to fulfill your recruitment requests. We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your submissions.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to update your submitted information, please email us at harshika@mscplacements.com.
