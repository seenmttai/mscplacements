Hiring Manager Guide

What is CA Industrial Training?

A CA Industrial Trainee is a final-year CA Final student who completes 9–12 months of their mandatory articleship training directly inside a corporate environment instead of an audit firm.

Employers gain highly analytical finance talent for nearly a full year, and many trainees convert smoothly to full-time roles upon qualifying.
